Security & compliance

National Archives Act — indexing follows the NARSSA methodology; retention and disposal schedules are held against every series.
National Key Points Act — restricted records are hidden from search for users without the clearance flag, and access attempts are logged.
POPIA — personal information in employee records is masked in reports unless the user holds the Human Capital scope.
Encryption — TLS 1.3 in transit, TDE at rest, checksums on every stored document.
Training — 3 administrator days and 5 user days included at go-live, refreshed annually.
